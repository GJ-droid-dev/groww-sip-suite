# User Research Interview Guide & Questionnaire: First-Time SIP Continuation

**Product:** Groww Mutual Funds & First-Time Investor Journey (Growth Team)  
**Target Segment:** Salaried Early-Career Investors (Age 22–27, earning ₹25k–₹55k/month, currently in Months 3–6 of their first SIP)  
**Methodology Standard:** NextLeap PM Knowledge Hub — *User Research & Innovation Framework* (Tag-Based Scope, Clarity Filter, Critical Incident Technique)  
**Privacy & Compliance:** 100% Zero collection of PAN, bank account numbers, or portfolio balances/screenshots.  
**Interview Format:** 45-minute 1:1 semi-structured video/audio interview.

---

## 1. Screener & Compliance Warm-up (3 Minutes)

### Screener Verification:
1. "Are you currently employed full-time or receiving a regular monthly salary?" *(Must be Yes)*
2. "Did you start your very first mutual fund SIP on Groww within the last 3 to 6 months?" *(Must be Yes)*
3. "Is your monthly SIP amount between ₹500 and ₹5,000?" *(Must be Yes)*

### Ethical & Privacy Opening Script (Mandatory):
> *"Hi [Name], thank you so much for taking the time to speak with me today! I'm researching how young professionals in India manage and experience their first investments.*  
> *There are no right or wrong answers—I'm here to learn about your real, unvarnished experiences. **A quick reminder on privacy:** please do not share your bank account numbers, PAN details, or exact investment balances. We will never ask for them, and you should never show your portfolio screen. Everything you share is strictly confidential and anonymized."*

### TAG 1: ONSET — The Initial Spark & Mental Anchors (8 Minutes)
*Anchor: What did they expect to happen, and who or what shaped their initial benchmark?*

1. **The Origin Story:**  
   *"Take me back to the week you decided to start your first SIP on Groww. What was happening around you? Who or what influenced you to take that first step?"*
2. **The Visualized Outcome:**  
   *"When you set that first monthly deduction (say, ₹1,000 or ₹2,000), what did you picture your investment looking like 6 months down the line? What did success look like in your mind?"*
3. **The Comparative Benchmark (Clarity: High):**  
   *"Before starting this SIP, where did your savings usually sit? (Bank savings, Fixed Deposit, cash)? When you think about whether your SIP is 'doing well', what do you compare it against?"*
   - *Probe:* *"Do you ever find yourself comparing it to a bank Fixed Deposit or a friend's returns?"*

---

### TAG 2: CADENCE & VISIBILITY — Dashboard Habits & Emotional Reactions (10 Minutes)
*Anchor: How do in-app metrics (NAV, returns, XIRR) make them feel, and how does checking behavior evolve?*

4. **Checking Frequency & Rhythm:**  
   *"In the first month, how often did you open Groww? How often do you open it now in Month 4 or 5?"*
   - *Probe:* *"What triggers you to open the app—a salary SMS, a news headline, an app notification, or just boredom?"*
5. **The First Screen Glance:**  
   *"When you open Groww and tap on 'Mutual Funds', walk me through where your eyes go first. What is the very first number or chart you look for?"*
6. **The Red Return Reaction (Clarity: High):**  
   *"Have you ever opened the app and seen your total returns or day's returns in RED or with a minus sign? Can you describe that exact moment? What went through your mind?"*
   - *Probe:* *"Did you feel like your principal money was shrinking? Did anyone explain why it was red?"*
7. **Metric Comprehension (XIRR vs Total Returns):**  
   *"On your dashboard, there are terms like 'Total Invested', 'Current Value', '1-Day Return', and 'XIRR'. Which of these make sense to you, and which ones do you ignore or find confusing?"*
   - *Probe:* *"If your app says XIRR -25%, what does that number mean to you personally?"*

---

### TAG 3: FRICTION & CASHFLOW — Critical Incident Deep-Dive (12 Minutes)
*Anchor (Assumed ⚠️): Did they pause/cancel because of market crashes, or because salary timing and bank penalties caused cashflow panic?*

8. **The Pause / Cancellation Moment (Critical Incident):**  
   *"Have you ever considered pausing, skipping, or cancelling your SIP? If yes, take me to the exact week that happened. What was happening in your personal life and bank account that week?"*
9. **Salary-to-Debit Timing Alignment:**  
   *"On what date of the month does your salary usually get credited into your account? And what date was your Groww SIP auto-debit scheduled for?"*
   - *Probe:* *"Has there ever been a month where salary arrived 2 or 3 days later than usual due to a weekend, bank holiday, or company payroll delay? What happened to your SIP debit?"*
10. **The Bank Penalty Experience (Clarity: High):**  
    *"Did your bank ever charge you an 'auto-debit return' or 'NACH bounce' penalty fee (like ₹250 or ₹295)? How did you find out about it, and what was your immediate reaction towards Groww and your bank?"*
11. **In-App Recourse Exploration:**  
    *"When money was tight that month, did you look for a way inside the Groww app to delay or skip just that one installment? What options did the app give you, and how did you feel about those options?"*

---

### TAG 4: EXTERNAL DRAFT & WORKAROUNDS — Outside-App Behaviors (7 Minutes)
*Anchor: What compensatory workarounds do they use outside Groww when feeling anxious or trapped?*

12. **The Net Banking Workaround:**  
    *"Have you ever logged into your bank’s net banking portal or UPI app to see or modify your Groww auto-pay mandate? Why did you do it there instead of inside Groww?"*
13. **External Validation & Family Dynamics (Clarity: High):**  
    *"When your portfolio was down or you felt unsure about your SIP, did you speak to anyone outside—a parent, colleague, or friend? What did they tell you?"*
    - *Probe:* *"Did any family member or elder advise you to stop mutual funds and stick to safe options like FDs, PPF, or gold?"*
14. **Online Communities (Reddit/YouTube):**  
    *"Do you ever search YouTube or read Reddit (like r/personalfinanceindia) when you are confused about mutual funds? What kind of videos or threads were you looking for?"*

---

### TAG 5: CONTINUITY & UNARTICULATED NEEDS — The Retention Levers (5 Minutes)
*Anchor (Clarity: Low): What would make a 12-month investing habit feel frictionless, resilient, and rewarding?*

15. **The Effortless Habit Vision:**  
    *"Think of a service you pay for every single month without ever thinking of cancelling (like your Netflix, mobile recharge, or rent). What makes continuing that service feel effortless compared to your SIP?"*
16. **Cashflow Flexibility:**  
    *"If Groww could detect when your salary actually arrived and automatically adjusted your debit date so you never faced a bank bounce fee, how would that change your confidence in staying invested?"*
17. **Reframing Dips (Unit Accumulation):**  
    *"During market dips, right now the app shows a red loss banner. What if, instead of showing a scary red number, the app showed you: 'Your ₹1,000 bought 15% more fund units this month at a discount'? How would that make you feel about continuing?"*
18. **Milestone Acknowledgment:**  
    *"If Groww celebrated milestones with you—like completing 3 months, 6 months, or 9 months of disciplined investing—would that make the journey feel more tangible? What kind of acknowledgment would matter to you?"*
